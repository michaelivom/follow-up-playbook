Target Picture

1 · Advisory Continuity

Continue the showroom consultation

The follow-up should feel like the natural next step after the visit: personal, useful, and directly connected to what was discussed in the showroom.

2 · Decision Support

Help the customer decide

The goal is not to chase leads. It is to reduce uncertainty around configuration, fabric, size, use case, delivery, and value.

3 · Consistent Performance

Standardize the system, not the human

Timing, structure, CRM logic, and core messages are standardized. Personal references and recommendations stay individual.

Success Factors

Operational success factors

  • CRM discipline: every visit gets a lead status: Hot, Warm, or Cold.
  • 24h first follow-up: no showroom visit should remain without a structured first message.
  • Personal reference: every message should include one real detail from the consultation.
  • Channel permission: WhatsApp only after explicit customer consent.
  • Advisor ownership: automation should create tasks and reminders, not replace the human relationship.

Quality success factors

  • One clear next step: each message should guide the customer gently.
  • Visual support: configuration links, sketches, photos, or fabric references whenever relevant.
  • Helpful, not needy: the tone should be calm, competent, and consultative.
  • Objection handling: address uncertainty around size, fabric, layout, delivery, and price early.
  • Review loop: sample 5 follow-ups per advisor per month and coach quality.

What to Avoid

Over-automation

Avoid robotic sequences that ignore the customer’s showroom conversation, readiness, and personal context.

Pushy urgency

Affordable-premium sofas are considered purchases. Pressure can reduce trust and damage the showroom experience.

Channel misuse

Do not use WhatsApp for long explanations, technical details, or repeated reminders. Keep it short and contextual.

Generic templates

A template without a personal line feels like marketing, not advisory support.

Weak handovers

If the showroom notes are poor, the follow-up will be poor. Capture configuration, needs, concerns, and next step.

No measurement

Without response rate, follow-up rate, timing, and close rate, the system cannot improve.
