Target Picture

Hot Leads

Decision Support

Concrete configuration, high buying intent, price discussed. Clarify final doubts and guide toward a confident decision.

Warm Leads

Guided Evaluation

Interested but still comparing, discussing internally, or refining needs. Support evaluation without pressure.

Cold Leads

Inspiration & Education

Early-stage visitors collecting ideas. Build trust, explain modularity, and offer low-friction planning support.

Success Factors

Operational success factors

  • Lead classification: every showroom visit must be marked as Hot, Warm, or Cold.
  • Different intent, different message: never use a decision template for an inspiration-stage customer.
  • 24h first touch: every lead receives a thoughtful first follow-up within one business day.
  • Channel permission: WhatsApp only after explicit customer consent.
  • Advisor ownership: automation should create tasks and reminders, not replace the human relationship.

Quality success factors

  • Hot: remove final friction and make the next step easy.
  • Warm: compare options and clarify decision criteria.
  • Cold: inspire, educate, and offer planning help without pushing for a configuration.
  • Personal reference: every message should include one real detail from the showroom conversation.
  • Review loop: sample 5 follow-ups per advisor per month and coach quality.

What to Avoid

One-size-fits-all

Cold leads should not receive the same configuration-heavy message as Hot leads.

Premature closing

Do not push inspiration-stage customers into a buying decision before they have clarity.

Channel misuse

Do not use WhatsApp for long explanations, technical details, or repeated reminders.
